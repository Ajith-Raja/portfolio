Frequently Asked Questions

What documents should I bring?

The documents required depend on your specific service. Generally, bring government-issued ID (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID), proof of address, and any service-specific documents. Contact us beforehand for a detailed checklist of required documents for your particular need.

How long will it take to complete my work?

Turnaround time varies based on the service type. Simple services like copying and printing take 1-2 hours. Document typing and forms usually take 1-2 days. Government-related services may take 3-7 days. We'll provide you with an accurate timeline when you submit your request.

Do you support online applications?

Yes! We provide comprehensive online services including government portals, e-service applications, certificate requests, and digital form submissions. Our experts can guide you through the entire online process, ensuring your applications are submitted correctly.

Are services available offline as well?

Absolutely! We offer both online and offline support. You can visit our physical center for document services, consultations, or offline work. We also support online services from home through email or phone consultations. Choose what works best for you.

What are your payment options?

We accept multiple payment methods including cash, digital payments (UPI, Debit/Credit cards), mobile wallets, and bank transfers. All prices are transparent with no hidden charges. Payment can be made during service or after completion, based on your preference.

Is my information confidential?

Yes, your confidentiality is our priority. All documents and personal information are handled with complete confidentiality and security. We comply with data protection regulations and ensure your documents are not shared without your explicit consent.
